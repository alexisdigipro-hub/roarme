Questions.

Is RoarMe free?

Yes. RoarMe and PROjects are free while in beta. There are no ads and nothing is sold.

Do I need a phone number?

No. You sign up with an email and pick an @name. A number is optional, only to find friends from your contacts.

What is PROjects?

A way to run a production inside a group: three chats with the right people in each, and a Workspace with media, files, locations, tasks, links, calls and the budget. Turn it on in Settings, PROjects.

What is the Production Hub?

The production manager of The Mad Lions. Link it with one key and a project stays the same on both sides; a project made in one makes its twin in the other.

Does the $ send money?

No. It records your fee or an expense, with its receipt, in the hub's finances and the project's budget. RoarMe moves no money.

Can I use it on several devices?

Yes. Your chats live in the cloud: sign in on a phone, a computer and the browser and everything is there.
