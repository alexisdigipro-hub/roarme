# RoarMe · instructions for Claude Code

RoarMe is a public chat app (working name), separate from the THEMADLIONS hub (repository
alexisdigipro-hub/themadlions-projects). Owner: Alex Konstantinidis, film director, not a developer.
Talk to him in Greek, friendly and concrete, no em dashes; the app's own text is English.

- React + Vite, plain CSS in src/styles.css, no UI framework. Dark glass look like the hub's chat:
  round glass buttons, pill fields, soft rounded tiles, the chosen item as lighter glass, orange accent.
- **Never a word with an underline as a button** (Alex, 5 Nov, for every site we make): actions are real buttons, a flat matte glass pill or a round glass icon.
- Data in Supabase: only the `roar_*` tables and the `roar-files` / `roar-avatars` buckets; never touch the
  hub's tables. Every database change goes in supabase/schema.sql, written so it can be run again.
- Desktop first (Alex, 15 Oct). Email + password sign-in, @username to be found.
- Pushing to main deploys to GitHub Pages; open a PR, wait for the green build, merge it yourself.
- Since 1 Nov 2026 RoarMe has no tie to the THEMADLIONS hub (the hub has its own chat); supabase/hub-unlink.sql removed
  the pieces (links, project-group guards, hub sign-in, roar_hub_* functions). Accounts and chats stay.
- Slack features (20 Oct): @all / @here / @role mentions (src/roles.jsx, supabase/mentions.sql: roar_roles, roar_mentioned,
  roar_my_mentions, roar_push_targets now with `mention`) reach people even in a muted chat; Remind Me on a message
  (src/reminders.jsx, supabase/reminders.sql + pg_cron 'roar-reminders', roar-push {reminder}); Tasks > Reminders.
- Quote (31 Oct, Alex from Telegram): words picked from a message ride at the top of the reply as "> " lines (src/format.jsx withQuote / splitQuote, read only on a reply); a computer selects them and gets the sel-bar (Copy, Quote), a phone uses the menu's Quote (QuotePick in src/Chat.jsx). No database change.
- One theme (Alex, 12 Nov: Simba, Velvet and Mango removed, then "delete Air, Stone and Halo"): Glass, in dark and light. It is built in three layers that all stay: styles.css (Stone's build, data-theme 'stone'), src/themes-halo.css (data-skin 'halo': the palette, bubbles, menus, windows, motion) and src/themes-glass.css (data-glass: the liquid glass on top). All three come with the app (main.jsx imports them in that order; index.html and prefs.js dressTheme put the three attributes on <html>); there is no theme picker. prefs.js getPrefs moves everyone to theme 'glass' (Halo's own look carries over; a colour or bubbles picked for Stone or Air are let go); haloish() is always true and wearTheme does nothing, both kept for the code that calls them. Where these notes say "Halo" or "Stone" they now mean those layers of Glass. styles.css still holds old rules for data-theme 'ember' and the mango / velvet / air skins; they match nothing and can be pruned. The Tasks panel (tasks.jsx, with calendar.jsx) is lazy; what the start needs of Tasks is src/taskCore.js.
- Tasks is off by default (Alex, 7 Nov): Settings > Features > Tasks (prefs.tasksTab, as callsTab) puts it in the bar at the foot and the phone's chat head; whoever already had tasks gets it on once (Chat.jsx).
- Settings menu (Alex, 12 Nov, tidied again: "not so many menus"): six doors in three boxes: 1 General · Appearance · Notifications and Sounds; 2 Chats · AllMedia; 4 Privacy and Security (SettingsPages.jsx PAGES, the last field is the group; TUCKED are pages without a door, SUBS the line under a door). General's page also shows Features and Language; Chats' page has doors to Chat Folders and Auto-Reply and shows Data and Storage; Privacy and Security also shows Active Sessions. The pages features / language / folders / autoreply / data / sessions still exist for links from elsewhere. RoarTransfer's own list is in AllMedia > Transfers > Sent by Me (media.jsx). Features holds what can be added or taken away (Calls tab with the call devices, Tasks); a new optional part goes there. Power Saving is in Appearance > Effects.
- Own chat wallpaper (Alex, 8 Nov): besides the built-ins, any colour ('color:#rrggbb') or one's own photo ('img:<url>|<opacity>', uploaded to roar-avatars/<id>/wall-*), in Settings > Appearance > Chat wallpaper (prefs.background) and per chat from ⋯ > Change Wallpaper (localStorage roarme-walls). src/walls.js ownColor / ownPhoto / photoValue / wallLook; extras.jsx WallTiles has the Colour and Your Photo tiles and the Photo Strength slider; CSS at the end of styles.css.
- Nothing of Roardino in RoarMe (Alex, 8 Nov): no Ordino (compose button, attach tile, Roardino window, ordino card; old ordino messages show as their text) and no ?embed=roardino mode (src/embed.js, the html.in-roardino look and the roardino:* messages are gone). Roardino's own table roardino_ordinos stays (it is Roardino's).
- Admin moderation (Alex, 8 Nov; supabase/admin-moderation.sql): an admin's own edits carry no "edited" (Chat.jsx amAdmin); roar_admin_delete_message deletes anyone's message for everyone (message menu: Delete for Everyone (Admin); RoarMe Inbox); roar_official_edit corrects only the official RoarMe account's text messages, quietly (RoarMe Inbox). Nobody can edit another person's words. Careful: roar_admin_delete (u uuid) is a different, older function that deletes a whole account.
- Blur (Alex, 9 Nov: "the heaviest thing, make it much lighter everywhere"): one knob, --blur (10px, phone 6px, no saturate) for every glass surface, and --blur-veil (4px, phone 3px) for full-screen veils (scrim, modal back, viewer, call, chat menu). No hard-coded blur(...) in backdrop-filter; no filter: blur in animations.
- Motion (Alex, 9 Nov: "they go and come back, choppy"): --spring is a smooth ease with no overshoot; the overshoot is only --pop (reactions, big emoji) and the long-press pop. Lists, pages and menus come in as one piece (no row-after-row stagger); one opening animation per thing; entrances short (.22 to .32 s) and small (scale .95 to .98, 6 to 14 px).
- Chat theme (Alex, 9 Nov; Settings > Appearance > Chat theme, SettingsPages.jsx ChatTheme): ready-made CHAT_THEMES (prefs.js: wallpaper + myBubble + theirBubble + accentHex) and one's own colours through Edit Your Theme (src/themeEditor.jsx: a sheet with the chat preview on top, Background / Accent / Messages (yours, theirs), its own colour field, #hex, Cancel / Set) plus Message Corners (bubbleR). applyLook sets --my-bubble / --their-bubble (+ -ink, chosen by inkOn) and --bubble-r with data-my-bubble / data-their-bubble / data-bubble-r; CSS at the end of styles.css, over every theme. Reset Appearance clears them.
- Folder tabs at the foot (Alex, 9 Nov; Halo since 12 Nov has a plain bar of words instead, themes-halo.css, wheelOn off for it; there the folder bar and the rail float over the list, which runs on under them by negative margins, --hfmb / --hpb; the same top bar on Contacts holds the search (.contacts-top, sticky) and on Calls the words CREATE NEW CALL (.new-call-bar)): no bar; a horizontal wheel that goes round (Chat.jsx wheeling / lightMid / toMiddleCopy / onWheelScroll; WHEEL_COPIES copies of the folders, the middle one real, a settled wheel jumps back to it unseen). The chosen pill never moves: .wheel-pill, its own lens in .wheel-box, stands in the middle and only takes the width of the word over it; a slide picks the folder that snaps to the middle (with a tick), both edges fade.
- Halo chat head and profile (Alex, 12 Nov): back, the name pill filling the row (weight 600: Safari blurs 650), the round picture; the pill and the picture open the profile, which in Halo is Telegram's (Profile.jsx pf-head / pf-hero / pf-back / pf-edit, call · video · mute · search · more; its Search reaches the chat through the window event roarme-find, and its More menu (wallpaper, Encrypted Chat, Create Group, Schedule Call, Export, Auto-Delete, Clear; nothing that Edit or the buttons beside it already do) through roarme-roomact, which Chat.jsx answers from roomActs).
- Voice and video messages as Telegram's (Alex, 12 Nov): a tap on the microphone swaps it with the camera (prefs local roarme-rec-mode); holding records (Chat.jsx holdStart: left 110px throws away, up 80px locks), voice as before, video a round message drawn square through a canvas (startVideo / flipCam / stopVideo, VideoRec overlay, at most a minute), sent as kind 'file' with file.round and dur and shown by extras.jsx NoteTile. Under 0.7 s nothing is sent.
- Halo's colours (Alex, 12 Nov, from a palette): Settings > Appearance > Accent Colour in Halo offers its orange and five of its own (prefs.js HALO_ACCENTS: navy, blue, lilac, rose, pink, each with a light form for the dark, a gradient and an ink); applyLook sets --halo-l / --halo-d / --halo-2 / --halo-mine(-top) / --halo-ink and data-halo-accent, themes-halo.css turns them into --halo. Halo's orange things use var(--halo) / --halo-mine, never a hard-coded orange.
- Halo's two foot bars alive (Alex, 12 Nov): the lens glides on a soft spring, the chosen sign pops, a pressed tab dips; a finger held on a bar swells the lens into glass with a rainbow rim that follows it and picks the tab under it on release (ui.jsx useLiquid, Chat.jsx on footLens and tabsLens; a bar that scrolls sideways keeps its scroll). Off with Power Saving and reduced motion.
- Halo in jelly (Alex, 12 Nov: "all the animations more jelly, many, in many places"): Halo alone leaves the calm Motion rule of 9 Nov. Last block of themes-halo.css: --spring / --pop overshoot there, jl-* keyframes for what comes in (messages, menus, windows, sheets, pages, the chat's head), a wobble for what is chosen, a squash for what is pressed. Off with Power Saving, Motion off and reduced motion; the other themes stay calm.
- Halo's motion tuned (Alex, 12 Nov: "more immediate and smoother, a little choppy"): in the jelly block only transform and opacity move, a press answers in 70 ms, entrances take .22 to .34 s with one small overshoot, nothing that mounts all the time is animated, and what pops or slides has no backdrop blur (sheet, message menu, toast). A deleted message dissolves and the gap closes (Chat.jsx vanish, styles.css .msg-out / msg-dust), in every theme.
- Lighter Halo, second pass (Alex, 12 Nov): full-screen veils do not fade in (a blurred layer whose opacity moves is blurred every frame), the foot bars have no backdrop blur, and a message's bubble carries what it is as classes (Chat.jsx kindCls: rt-b, aud-b, file-b, solo; with pic, loc-b, art-b, event-b, note-b) so themes style messages without :has(). Keep new message rules on those classes.
- Halo's pop-ups are two families, the same everywhere (Alex, 12 Nov; last block of themes-halo.css): menus (.menu, .menu-list, .rx-pick, .emoji-pick, .mention-pick, .toast, .vw-menu) are white frosted glass at 80% with dark words, 24px corners (dark grey glass in dark mode; Alex first had them dark with grain, then chose white); windows and sheets (.modal, .as-sheet, .te-sheet) are light frosted glass at 88% with a bright rim, 30px corners, solid when they fill a phone's screen. A new menu or window takes one of the two; do not give it a look of its own.
- Photo editor before sending (Alex, 12 Nov, after Telegram's): in the attach sheet a tap on a picked photo (or its pencil) opens src/photoEdit.jsx: Turn, Crop to a shape (Square, 4:5, 16:9, centred), Draw (six inks, Undo), Adjust (Light, Contrast, Colour). - Sliding to get about on a phone (Alex, 12 Nov: "every window slides right and left, wherever it can"): pages in .main already go back with a slide to the right (Chat.jsx, presses the page's [data-back]); every window (ui.jsx Modal, useSwipeClose) follows a slide to the right and closes; the attach sheet is pulled down by its top ([data-grab]) and slides between Gallery, File and Audio; the profile's tabs and AllMedia's tabs change with a slide (useTabSwipe; data-tabs-swipe 'mid' keeps the close / back slide off while not on the first tab). Things that move sideways themselves (fields, sliders, maps, canvases, rows of tabs, sideways scrollers) are left alone.
- Glass (Alex, 12 Nov: "take Halo and make a theme all in liquid glass, the menu thick with words but our icons, the folders at the top"): a fourth theme, 'glass', Halo's build with data-glass on <html> (prefs.js dressTheme; wearTheme loads themes-halo.css and then src/themes-glass.css, whose rules start html:root:root[data-glass] so they win over Halo's). prefs.js haloish(t) is true for Halo and Glass: ask it wherever the code asked for Halo (accents, wallpapers, app background, the preview's bars). - Halo's page wears the chats' wallpaper (Alex, 12 Nov: "the overall background follows the one chosen for the chats"): App.jsx gives the body class app-wall and what a chat wears (data-bg, data-img, data-own, data-wop and the --wall vars, from walls.js wallLook of prefs.appBackground, or of prefs.background while that is '': Settings > Appearance > App background has Same as Chats and, with it off, its own tiles); themes-halo.css draws it (the wallpaper washes also name body.app-wall; last block for colour, photo and strength). - Before every push (12 Nov, after Settings took the whole app down: a deleted name, THEMES, was still used, and neither the build nor a syntax check sees that): run eslint's no-undef over src (the global eslint in /opt/node22 with a flat config that only sets browser globals and 'no-undef': 'error') and push only when it names nothing new. When something is deleted, grep the whole of src for its name, not just for the pattern being removed.
- GLASS TRAVEL (Alex, 12 Nov: "keep this animation in memory, we will need it"; it began as "the animation the bar at the foot of the Claude app has", with its RGB split): the app's signature motion, by this name. A lens moving between choices goes in two beats: .lens-go, 150 ms on cubic-bezier(.3, .7, .4, 1): it lifts into clearer glass (scale about 1.03 by 1.16), stretches from the old choice across to the new one, and the colours split at its rim (thin shadows: red left, blue right, a little green above, yellow below); then .lens-land, 300 ms on cubic-bezier(.25, 1.45, .45, 1): it draws in onto the new choice and settles, the split fading; classes off at 470 ms. Only transform, width and box-shadow move. ui.jsx useLens does it for any bar with [data-travel] (the menu at the foot, the folders' bar, every Lens); the look is in themes-glass.css (.foot.lens.lens-go and the GLASS TRAVEL block). Off with Power Saving, Motion off and reduced motion (calm()). GLASS PRESS is its small sister with the same response (in at once, out on the same spring): the split colours at the rim of what a finger is on (round buttons, Send, the name's pill, sheet and profile tabs, a switch's knob that stretches). Since the same day it is also on everything a long press works on: a message, photo or round video and a chat's row (.pressing from Chat.jsx), a folder's tab, the record button, a contact's row. Three more of the family, same response (themes-glass.css, last block): GLASS MORPH (gl-morph: what opens grows out of the pressed place as a round drop with the split rim: .menu, .msg-menu, .emoji-pick, .mention-pick, the attach sheet), GLASS MERGE (gl-bud: Send buds out of the writing box and the microphone comes back the same way) and GLASS DROP (gl-drop / gl-rise / gl-bead / gl-toast: an arriving message, the unread counts, a notice). Names kept for later, not built: Glass Shine, Tilt, Glow, Lift, Ripple, Scroll, Pull. When Alex asks for "Glass Travel" or "the same response" somewhere, use these timings and curves, not new ones.

- Glass on a screen of ordinary sharpness (Alex's desktop PC with a 2K monitor, 12 Nov: "a little pixelated, the phone is finer and more shiny"): last block of themes-glass.css, @media (max-resolution: 1.5dppx): a finer, fainter --grain, thinner and softer split-colour rims on Glass Travel and Glass Press, and --blur at 6px as on the phone. Details one pixel wide are made on an iPhone (3 dots a pixel); anything new of that kind (grain, hairlines, rims) gets its softer form there too.
